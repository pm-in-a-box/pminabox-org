Privacy Policy

Effective August 11, 2026

1. The short version

The public website is static and does not use advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies. The PM in a Box product is a hosted service. It stores and processes the account, workspace, connector, and operational data needed to provide the features a customer uses. We do not sell personal information.

2. Who we are and what this policy covers

PM in a Box is built and operated by APM Help. This policy covers pm-in-a-box.org, the hosted service, the in-app marketplace, support, and official client software if and when we release it. The public website is separate from the hosted Product. The PM in a Box desktop application is not publicly released as of this policy's effective date. A customer agreement may include additional terms for a specific Box.

3. The public website

pm-in-a-box.org has no account system, advertising, analytics script, or application cookie. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process request data such as IP address and user agent for security and operations under the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you email us or interact with the public repository, we receive the information you choose to provide through that service.

4. Data the product processes

Depending on the features a customer enables, PM in a Box processes:

  • Account data. Name, email address, Box membership, role, department access, sign-in records, device state, and notification settings, including whether policy-change email notices are enabled.
  • Workspace content. Shared and private chat, tasks, comments, files, email, calendar events, automations, agent and skill configuration, Box Apps, website projects, and related metadata.
  • Connected business data. Records imported, mirrored, searched, reported on, or acted on through an authorized connector.
  • Credentials and capabilities. Encrypted connector credentials, OAuth tokens, client sessions, expiring download links, and hashed or encrypted access tokens required to run the service.
  • Usage and operations data. Model token usage, agent-run counts, connection and sync status, integrity records, audit logs, errors, and product activity described below.

5. Where service data is stored

PM in a Box stores operational service data in its Cloudflare account. Cloudflare services used by the product include Workers, D1, R2, Durable Objects, Queues, Vectorize, Workers AI, Pages, Email Sending, and RealtimeKit. The customer organization owns its chats, files, configuration, and imported business records. PM in a Box processes that data to provide the service.

If and when an official desktop application is released and installed, it may keep selected local state, including unsent drafts, the signed-in session, device and extension state, expiring export links, and local skill source files. A local skill or file would enter cloud storage when a user saves it into the Box or uploads it.

6. AI and connected services

Agent requests may send the prompt, relevant conversation context, selected attachments, and connector results to OpenRouter and the model provider selected for the department or Personal AI. Cloudflare Workers AI is also used for functions such as embeddings, transcription, and pinned utility models.

When a user authorizes a connector, PM in a Box sends requests to that provider within the configured scope. Current connectors include AppFolio, Rentvine, RealPage, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, HubSpot, QuickBooks Online, and PandaDoc. Those providers process data under their own terms. Voice calls use Cloudflare RealtimeKit.

The Chrome companion receives browser commands only after it is paired to a user. It can navigate, list and focus tabs, click, type, capture screenshots, and extract visible page text when an authorized computer-use request calls those capabilities.

7. Analytics and support traces

Product analytics use bounded records for workspace loads, authenticated connections, human chat activity, agent lifecycle, token consumption, and agent runs. Analytics tables store IDs, dates, timestamps, and counters. They do not store message text, attachments, connector payloads, or credentials. Product activity records are retained for up to 400 days.

Each completed agent reply receives a support debug ID. The related trace may include the triggering message, model turns, tool arguments, bounded result previews, final reply, and error details. Traces can contain customer content and are retained for up to 30 days. Support cannot browse traces by Box. An authorized staff member needs the exact debug ID, and cross-Box access is logged. Sharing a debug ID with support allows us to retrieve that specific trace.

8. How we use data

  • Provide, secure, maintain, and improve the service.
  • Authenticate users and enforce Box, department, personal, and app permissions.
  • Run requested agents, connectors, automations, reports, exports, apps, calls, and website builds.
  • Send sign-in, invite, notification, support, and service messages.
  • Measure product adoption and capacity without copying message content into analytics tables.
  • Investigate a specific problem when a customer provides its debug ID or asks for support.
  • Comply with law and protect customers, users, the service, and the public.

9. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We disclose data to service providers that process it for PM in a Box, to connected providers a user authorizes, within a customer organization according to product permissions, when a customer directs us to, or when required by law. Marketplace authors do not receive the installing Box's content or connector credentials through a listing install.

Marketplace Content submitted for publication is intended for distribution and may be visible to other users together with the submitter's display name, listing metadata, documentation, and update history. Do not include private credentials, confidential information, or personal information that you do not have the right to publish. The Terms of Use and License Agreement describes the distribution license granted for marketplace submissions.

10. Access, export, retention, and deletion

Box owners can create a portable archive of shared Box data without support intervention. The owner-wide archive excludes Personal AI, personal connector data, direct messages, private group chats, connector secrets, session capabilities, and MCP tokens.

Retention varies by data type and operational need. Some records have explicit limits described in this policy. Other service data remains while the Box is active or as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, secure the system, and meet legal obligations. To request access, correction, export, or deletion, contact us at robert@apmhelp.com. We may need to verify the request and the requester's authority for the Box.

11. Security

PM in a Box uses tenant and department boundaries, server-side credentials, encrypted connector secrets, signed or bearer capabilities, scoped exports, audit records, and bounded agent context. No system can guarantee absolute security. Customers remain responsible for choosing appropriate access, reviewing agent output, and protecting the accounts and systems they connect.

12. Children

The Product is not directed to people under 18. We do not knowingly permit a person under 18 to create a Product account.

13. Changes and contact

We may update this policy at any time. The current version and effective date will be posted on this website, and you should check back for updates. Unless a notice states otherwise, an update applies when posted and applies prospectively. If applicable law requires advance notice or renewed consent, we will provide it.

If you have registered for the hosted version and enabled policy-change email notifications, we will send a notice to the email address on your account at the time an update is posted. Delivery may be delayed or fail, and the posted policy controls unless applicable law says otherwise. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to robert@apmhelp.com or mailed to APM Help, P.O. Box 582, Houston, TX 77001. See also the Terms of Use and License Agreement.
